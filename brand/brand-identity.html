01 · Brand essence

We won’t leave you hangin’.

Babalas Rescue+ is a hangover-recovery brand made in South Africa. “Babalas” is local slang for the morning after, and the logo says the rest: a figure dangling from a cliff edge, one hand still holding on, and a product that helps them climb back up.

The brand sits between functional recovery (electrolytes, B-vitamins, antioxidants) and a party-culture sense of humour. It is the friend who shows up with water and no judgement. Its promise is a single line, used on every pack:

bounce back from your hangover

The idea in one frame

A cliff and a climber. The cliff is last night. The climber is you, mid-recovery, hanging on and about to bounce back. The + in “rescue+” is the help arriving.

Hangover recovery Rehydrate & restore Boost energy Boost immunity Relieve “hangxiety”

Benefit claims are taken from the client trade presenters. All creative must carry the responsible-drinking message (see Voice).

03 · Voice & tone

Funny, but the brand is always the solution, never the punchline.

These three rules come straight from the client’s influencer content brief and apply to all copy, captions and scripts.

1

Self-aware & honest

We know what last night was. No shaming, no miracle claims. Say what the product does, plainly.

2

Distinctly South African

Local slang, local places, local humour. Written how people actually talk, not translated from an overseas template.

3

Funny, never at our own expense

The joke is the hangover. The brand arrives as the fix, so it is never the butt of the gag.

04 · Logo

One mark, two parts.

The lockup combines the climber icon with the babalas rescue+ wordmark and tagline. The wordmark is drawn artwork: never retype it in a font. Always use the supplied files in brand/logo/.

Primary: yellow on Babalas Blue (as seen on the cans and sachets). Secondary: blue on Rescue Yellow. Single-colour yellow / white / blue / dark versions are for photography overlays and one-colour print. Pink is not used for the logo.

Clear space

Keep at least the width of the “+” clear on every side (shown by the dashed padding). No type, edges or imagery inside it. The icon may bleed off the canvas on its own (see Graphic language) but the full lockup never does.

Minimum size

Full lockup: 110 px / 30 mm wide minimum (the tagline is legible down to there). Below that, use the icon alone. Wordmark-only: 90 px / 25 mm.

Font note. Three different font lists exist in the client’s influencer-brief files: Futura + Montserrat (the branded version on the Products folder, dated Oct 2025), Century Gothic (an earlier version) and Poppins (a re-used Vitality I.V. template with a different orange palette). We take the Futura + Montserrat brief as authoritative: it is the only one on the Babalas cover artwork. Century Gothic is a safe system fallback. Poppins and the orange palette should not be used.

The tagline treatment alternates weights: bounce back (bold) · from your (regular) · hangover (bold), exactly as in the logo lockup.

07 · Graphic language

Four moves that make it feel like Babalas.

1 · Hang the climber off the edge

Crop the icon huge and let it bleed off the canvas, as on the influencer brief cover. The cliff becomes the layout edge and the climber is always mid-frame.

2 · Split along the cliff

Blue and yellow meet on a jagged diagonal, never a soft gradient. Flip which colour is “rock” and which is “sky” to keep layouts fresh.

+

3 · The “+” is the rescue

Pull the plus from “rescue+” out as a badge, bullet or sticker. It marks the helpful bit: benefits, offers, “new”.

bounce back
from your
hangover

4 · Tagline as a stamp

Use the bold / regular tagline as a repeatable lockup under products, at the end of videos and on stickers.

Shape

Round corners (20–32 px), pill buttons and tags. Sharp points are reserved for the cliff edge.

Space

Generous. Big headline, one product, one tagline. Let the colour block do the work.

Don’t

No gradients between brand colours, no stock-wellness icons, no neon-on-neon, no extra colours (especially the orange ramp from the Poppins template).
