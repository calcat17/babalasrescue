# Babalas Rescue+ — brand quick reference (v1.0)

**What it is:** South African hangover-recovery brand. Energy can (300 ml, 100 mg caffeine, lightly carbonated) and caffeine-free sachets (13,4 g). "Babalas" = local slang for a hangover.
**Promise:** *bounce back from your hangover.* **Line:** *we won't leave you hangin'.*
**Idea:** a climber hanging off a cliff edge; the "+" in rescue+ is the help arriving.

## Voice (from the client's influencer brief)
1. Self-aware and honest. 2. Distinctly South African. 3. Funny, but the brand is always the solution, never the punchline.
Never make medical claims or encourage excess. Include "We support responsible drinking"; not for under-18s, pregnant or lactating women.

## Colour
| Role | Name | HEX | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Babalas Blue | `#2CACEF` | field colour |
| Primary | Rescue Yellow | `#FDEA12` | signature |
| Accent | Hangover Pink | `#F6009D` | sparingly (e.g. "ENERGY" label) |
| Derived | Night | `#0B2A40` | all small text; 5.8:1 on blue, 11.9:1 on yellow |
| Derived | Deep Blue | `#0F5F8F` | blue text on white (6.9:1) |

Yellow on blue is only **2.05:1**: logo and large display type only, never body copy. Pink on blue is never used. Rough proportion: blue 45 / yellow 35 / white 15 / pink 5.

## Type
Headlines **Futura Heavy** (web stand-in **Jost 800**), body **Montserrat**. The wordmark is artwork: never retype it. Ignore the Poppins/orange template (a re-used Vitality I.V. file).
Tagline treatment: **bounce back** from your **hangover** (bold / regular / bold).

## Logo
Files in `brand/logo/`. Default: yellow on blue. Secondary: blue on yellow. Over photos: single-colour yellow or white. Clear space = width of the "+". Min width 110 px / 30 mm (icon alone below that). No pink logo, stretching, shadows or low-contrast backgrounds.

## Graphic language
1. Oversized climber icon bleeding off the canvas. 2. Blue/yellow split on a jagged diagonal. 3. The "+" as badge/bullet. 4. Bold/regular tagline as a stamp. Round corners (20–32 px); no gradients between brand colours.

## Video
9:16, HD, well lit. Short 5–15 s, medium 15–30 s; influencer Reel/TikTok 30–45 s. Product (can and bottle) clearly in frame.

## Audience
Social adult drinkers (18+); marketing focus moving towards Black South African consumers (client brief, Jan 2026).
